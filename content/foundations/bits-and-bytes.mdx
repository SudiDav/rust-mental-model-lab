# Bits and Bytes

Computers store patterns of bits. The pattern does not carry a single built-in meaning; a program decides whether to interpret it as a number, a character, or something else.

<Concept>One byte is eight bits. The same eight bits can be interpreted in different ways.</Concept>

<MentalModel>Think of a byte as eight tiny switches. The switches are the data; the interpretation is the lens you choose.</MentalModel>
